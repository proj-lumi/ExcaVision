# ExcaVision brand guidelines

These colors are non-negotiable for ExcaVision public and customer-facing surfaces.
They are intended to feel practical, calm, and trustworthy for people making site
and infrastructure decisions.

## Brand palette

| Token | Light value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep marine | `#12343B` | Primary dark surface, navigation CTA, system sections |
| Ink | `#193138` | Headings, primary text, wordmark |
| Ink soft | `#476067` | Supporting text |
| Muted | `#64777C` | Hints, secondary labels, inactive steps |
| Paper | `#F2F6F4` | Main page background |
| Panel | `#FBFCFA` | Cards, forms, navigation background |
| Line | `#D5E0DF` | Borders and dividers |
| Safety orange | `#D96B2F` | The single brand accent, primary actions, selected states |
| Safety orange deep | `#A9471E` | Hover and pressed accent states |
| Safety orange soft | `#FFF0E7` | Selected and highlighted surfaces |
| Operational green | `#19705D` | Healthy, success, and acknowledged states |
| Operational green soft | `#E3F4ED` | Healthy and success surfaces |

## Non-negotiable rules

- Use safety orange as the only brand accent.
- Do not add AI-purple, neon cyan, random blue, or decorative gradients.
- Do not use pure black or pure white as page colors.
- Use operational green only for real system state, success, or health.
- Use red only for errors or safety-critical warning states, never as branding.
- The ExcaVision logo is a typographic wordmark: `ExcaVision`. Do not add a generated icon, mascot, circuit mark, or decorative badge.
- Prefer real site photography or clearly labeled image placeholders. Do not use generic AI-generated excavation images.
- Keep corners consistently soft. Use the established 8px input, 10px control, 12px panel, and 20px primary request-panel scale.
